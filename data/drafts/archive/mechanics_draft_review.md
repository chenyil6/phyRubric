# Mechanics caption rewrite draft — changed records only

Rules: **person** = whole person removed or shrunk to the body part that must apply the force (passive voice, a device or the environment as cause; a load / target / occluded body swapped for an object or animal) · **expectation** = expectation re-worded to match, not loosened · **keep_person** = the body is the phenomenon, left as is · **scene** = scene swapped at the user's request for one that tests the same physics. The human action, where it survives, sits in the opening clause so the judged physics happens after the person has exited. Unchanged records (no person to begin with) are omitted here but present in the JSON.
